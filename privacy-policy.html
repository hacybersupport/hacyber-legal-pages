Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Document Ref: HGT-PRIVACY-v2

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

In Short: We collect personal information you provide to us alongside limited data from verified third-party platforms.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when registering on our Services, expressing interest in obtaining details about our solutions, or contacting our support team directly.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We process your information to deliver, manage, and optimize our services, handle security prevention, and stay compliant with legal frameworks.
  • Account Management: Facilitating secure login authentication and maintenance.
  • Service Delivery: Providing requested onboarding, integration support, and platform services.
  • Customer Support: Resolving user issues and monitoring system status.
  • Transaction Handling: Fulfilling and processing service activations and billing.

15. THIRD-PARTY PLATFORMS AND ACCOUNT INFORMATION

When providing support or services related to third-party gig platforms or delivery applications, we only request or process account information necessary to assist you with onboarding, verification, or service execution.

HACYBERGLOBALTECH is an independent entity and is not directly affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by third-party gig platforms unless specifically stated. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of our support services complies with the terms and conditions of their respective platforms.

16. PAYMENT INFORMATION AND STRIPE

All payments processed through our platform use secure third-party payment gateways such as Stripe. HACYBERGLOBALTECH does not store complete credit or debit card numbers on our servers. All transaction data handled by third-party payment processors is subject to their privacy policies, security standards, and terms of service.

17. DATA SECURITY AND RETENTION

We apply industry-standard security protocols to protect all user data, including personal identifiers and gig platform details. Data retained for legal, financial, or administrative records will be securely archived and isolated until it is no longer needed, at which point it will be permanently deleted or anonymized.

18. DO WE MAKE UPDATES TO THIS NOTICE?

In Short: Yes, we will update this notice as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws.

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date at the top of this document.
